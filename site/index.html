Physical replication

Max gp of this basket one account could assemble per day under buy limits. There is no sell-side limit: entry is capped, exit is not.

What this is

Four equal-weighted, liquidity-capped baskets of OSRS items. Membership is decided by liquidity rank with entry/exit buffers, so an item drifting around the boundary is left alone rather than traded every review.

Valuation uses volume-weighted hourly buckets, never the /latest endpoint — whose median staleness is 37 minutes and which reports crossed prices on 15.6% of items. An index built on /latest can be moved by a single transaction for about a third of a US cent.
